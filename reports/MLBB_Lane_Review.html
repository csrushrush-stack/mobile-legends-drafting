What the current MPL data says, lane by lane · 425 games

No tiers. A hero is not graded S or A here. The order is the draft app's own formula: win rate above 50 scaled by how much the sample can be trusted, plus how contested the hero is. Win rate is the pooled professional record across the selected leagues, Games is how many times it was picked, Bans how often it was denied, and Presence is picks + bans over total games. Rank win and Rank ban are the publisher's Mythical Glory+ ladder figures, shown for contrast — the ladder and tournament play disagree regularly.
