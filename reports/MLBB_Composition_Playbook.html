From Duo to Full Five

How to turn a good hero pair into a working team — composition archetypes, measured trios, real 5-man drafts from the 2026 Asian Games, and the macro to run them.

You were right, and the data proves it. A duo is 2 of 5 slots. When I re-ran the Asian Games pair data looking for triangles — three heroes where all three pairs were actually played together — 31 trios fell out, and they are far more predictive than the duos. The single best-sampled core in the whole tournament was Aulus + Hirara + Obsidia: the three component pairs went 4–1, 4–1 and 4–0, a combined 12–2 (85.7%). No duo in the dataset is that stable. Everything below builds the duo out into a full five.

01Why the duo is only a third of the answer

Every MLBB team has exactly five jobs. A duo covers two of them, and often the same one twice — which is how good duos turn into bad drafts.

EXP
Absorbs pressure alone, becomes the second frontline late
Jungle
Owns the tempo, the objectives, and the gold curve
Mid
Waveclear, rotation speed, the magic damage source
Gold
The sustained damage that ends the game
Roam
Vision, engage or peel, and the comp's identity

The three failure modes of a "good duo"

  • Slot collision. Two sustain heroes win lane and then lose the game — you have no damage. Two carries have damage and no frontline, so any dive deletes you. A duo that both want the same job is a duo that costs you a lane.
  • Missing enabler. A carry needs someone to hold the enemy still. Obsidia is a fine marksman, but she only becomes a tournament-winning pick once there is a frontline to stand behind — which is exactly why every top AG2026 trio contains a fighter and a roam alongside the carry.
  • Wrong archetype. Eudora + Atlas and Fredrinn + Angela are both excellent pairs that want opposite games. Play the first slowly and you lose; play the second aggressively and you lose. The pair is not the plan — the archetype is the plan.

What the trios tell you

  • Aulus + Hirara + Obsidia (12–2, 85.7%, min pair 4 games) is the most trustworthy core in the dataset — an EXP fighter, an assassin jungler and a gold-lane marksman. It leaves Mid and Roam open, which is exactly where you should be flexible.
  • Atlas + Hirara + Obsidia (10–1, 90.9%) swaps the fighter for a lockdown roam. Same carry, same jungler, different frontline — and it still worked. That is the sign of a genuine core rather than a coincidence.
  • Aulus + Eudora + Minotaur (10–1) and Aulus + Gloo + Obsidia (10–1) show the two ways to build around Aulus: sustain-and-outlast, or frontline-and-protect.
  • Every single top trio contains a gold-lane carry. Not one is three fighters or three mages. The carry is the constant; everything else is scaffolding.
  • The bad trios are instructive. Eudora + Paquito + Uranus went 8–13 (36.4%) — three heroes that all want to fight up close, with no one to hold the enemy in place and no sustained damage.

Worked example — building out Hirara + Obsidia

The pair went 4–0 (100%). It covers Jungle + Gold. Three measured thirds exist:

Option A — Aulus (EXP)
Trio 12–2, 85.7% on a 4-game minimum. The safest pick. Leaves Mid + Roam open for a mage and a tank.
Option B — Atlas (Roam)
Trio 10–1, 90.9%. Higher ceiling, smaller sample. Locks the roam slot into a hard-engage identity.
Option C — Gloo (Roam)
Trio 10–2, 83.3%. The comp Myanmar actually ran to win gold — durable frontline that disrupts positioning.

Then fill the last two slots by archetype. With Option A you have EXP + Jungle + Gold, so you need Mid + Roam. Because Aulus + Hirara + Obsidia is a sustain-frontline shell, the correct fills are a scaling or burst mage (Zhuxin, Eudora, Valentina, Cecilion) and a healing or tanky roam (Minotaur, Estes, Rafaela, Carmilla). Full comp: Aulus · Hirara · Zhuxin · Obsidia · Minotaur — and Myanmar's actual gold-medal game 2 was the same shape with Gloo on roam instead.

04The six archetypes — pick one before you pick heroes

A composition is not five good heroes. It is one plan, staffed by five heroes. Choose the archetype first, then draft heroes that serve it. These are the six that show up in real MLBB, each with the slot template, the win condition, the macro and what beats it.

1

Setup + AOE Wombo / Lock-and-Load

One hero locks several enemies in place; the rest dump area damage on the pile. Every fight is won in a single 1.5-second window.

Win condition Force one decisive 5v5. You do not want a long game — you want one clean teamfight at an objective.
Roam
Atlas, Tigreal, Khufra, Lolita, Minotaur, Franco
Mid
Eudora, Cecilion, Gord, Lylia, Yve, Vexana, Pharsa
Jungle
Nolan, Hayabusa, Lancelot, Gusion, Harley, Suyou
EXP
Aulus, Paquito, Esmeralda, Barats, Arlott
Gold
Obsidia, Claude, Brody, Moskov, Bruno
How to play it (macro)
  • Group from minute 3. Do not trade solo kills — you want the enemy grouped, not scattered.
  • Stage 20–30 seconds before Turtle or Lord spawns. Position so the enemy must walk into your lockdown.
  • Roam holds Flicker + Ultimate. Never burn the engage to chase a kill.
  • Take the fight only when the enemy has 3+ heroes within one screen. Otherwise walk away.
  • After a won fight, immediately convert to turret or Lord. Wombo comps decay if the game drags.
Core pairs seen in the data

Atlas + Cecilion · Eudora + Atlas · Tigreal + Gord · Atlas + Lylia · Khufra + Xavier

Loses to

Split-push comps (you can never force the fight), Diggie (his Ultimate strips the crowd control), and anything that baits your cooldowns then re-engages.

Tournament evidence

AG2026: Eudora + Atlas was played 6 times (4–2). Atlas and Carmilla were the two most-banned heroes in the tournament at 19 bans each — teams feared exactly this pattern.

2

Sustain / Attrition Out-last

Nobody dies. You out-heal, out-shield and out-tank until the enemy runs out of resources and has to walk into you.

Win condition Extend every fight past 8–10 seconds. Value accrues with time; the longer it goes, the more you win.
Roam
Estes, Angela, Floryn, Rafaela, Carmilla, Kalea
Mid
Valentina, Harith, Cecilion, Zhuxin, Yve
Jungle
Julian, Fredrinn, Hirara, Hayabusa, Akai
EXP
Aulus, Yu Zhong, Esmeralda, Terizla, Uranus, Barats
Gold
Obsidia, Claude, Moskov, Brody, Irithel
How to play it (macro)
  • Play slow. Do not force fights before minute 8 — your power curve starts late.
  • Take every even objective trade. You win the long game, so a 1-for-1 swap is always good for you.
  • Keep the healer behind the frontline, never on the engage. Losing the healer loses the comp.
  • Bait the enemy into committing on your tank, then heal through the burst.
  • Win condition arrives at 3 items and after the first Lord. Do not panic if you are 3k gold behind at 10 min.
Core pairs seen in the data

Fredrinn + Angela · Yu Zhong + Angela · Terizla + Floryn · Julian + Estes · Harith + Floryn

Loses to

Burst that kills before healing matters (Eudora, Gusion, Lancelot), anti-heal itemisation (Necklace of Durance, Dominance Ice), and pure-pick comps that delete your healer first.

Tournament evidence

AG2026: the winning trio Aulus + Eudora + Minotaur (10–1) and Aulus + Minotaur + Valentina (9–1) are both sustain shells. Minotaur alone went 3–0 with each of Eudora, Nolan and Valentina.

3

Pick / Dive Assassinate

Delete one target instantly, then play the rest of the fight 5v4. You never want a fair fight.

Win condition Isolate and remove a carry in rotation, snowball the gold lead, then force objectives 5v4.
Roam
Mathilda, Angela, Chip, Kalea, Marcel
Mid
Eudora, Zhuxin, Selena, Kadita, Valentina
Jungle
Nolan, Ling, Hayabusa, Fanny, Lancelot, Harley, Suyou
EXP
Paquito, Arlott, Badang, Masha, Chou
Gold
Claude, Brody, Moskov, Yi Sun-shin
How to play it (macro)
  • Invade the enemy jungle on the first buff. You want to be ahead before anyone has Ultimate.
  • Track the enemy carry's position constantly — your whole comp is aimed at one player.
  • Force fights near walls and bushes. Open ground is your enemy; corners are your friend.
  • Convert every pick into an objective immediately. A kill with no objective is wasted.
  • Never start a 5v5. If the enemy is grouped, split and look for the isolated target instead.
Core pairs seen in the data

Ling + Mathilda · Hayabusa + Mathilda · Nolan + Angela · Fanny + Mathilda

Loses to

Tight grouping, strong peel roams (Lolita, Diggie, Khufra), and being behind early — a dive comp that is not ahead has no way to win.

Tournament evidence

AG2026: Nolan was the most-picked hero (23 of 32 games) and Hirara had 15 bans. Both are dive junglers. But Nolan finished 47.83% — the most-picked hero was not the most successful one.

4

Front-to-Back Protect the Carry

One unkillable front line, one shielded hyper-carry. The carry cannot be reached, and eventually cannot be stopped.

Win condition Survive to 12+ minutes. Once the marksman has 3 items, the fight is over.
Roam
Lolita, Edith, Angela, Rafaela, Chip, Khufra
Mid
Yve, Xavier, Pharsa, Novaria, Zhuxin
Jungle
Fredrinn, Barats, Akai, Hylos
EXP
Barats, Uranus, Gatotkaca, Hilda, Edith
Gold
Moskov, Claude, Brody, Beatrix, Melissa, Ixia
How to play it (macro)
  • Concede early. Farm safely, do not contest the first Turtle if it costs a life.
  • Defend turrets rather than chasing kills. Your job is to reach the item spike.
  • The roam's only job is to body-block for the carry. Never leave them alone.
  • Group at 3 items and walk down mid as a single unit.
  • Front line stays between the enemy and the carry at all times — never flank.
Core pairs seen in the data

Lolita + Moskov · Melissa + Lolita · Brody + Tigreal · Claude + Rafaela

Loses to

Dive and assassinate comps that bypass the front line, and losing all outer turrets before the carry spikes.

Tournament evidence

AG2026: Obsidia (a marksman) posted 60% across 15 picks and appeared in the two best trios in the tournament. Every top trio in the data contains a gold-lane carry protected by a fighter and a roam.

5

Poke / Siege Chip and Zone

Never commit. Chip them down from range, take space for free, and let the enemy bleed out.

Win condition Take turrets without fighting. Force the enemy to walk into your range or lose the map.
Roam
Diggie, Khufra, Lolita, Atlas, Kalea
Mid
Yve, Pharsa, Novaria, Xavier, Zhuxin, Cecilion
Jungle
Kimmy, Yi Sun-shin, Sora, Nolan
EXP
Aulus, Uranus, Esmeralda, Ruby, Barats
Gold
Brody, Clint, Kimmy, Lesley, Ixia
How to play it (macro)
  • Hold mid and never cross the river without vision.
  • Poke the enemy off the turret, then take it. Repeat lane by lane.
  • Never chase. If the enemy retreats, take the objective, not the kill.
  • Use zone-control Ultimates (Yve, Pharsa) to deny the objective area entirely.
  • Stay spread — a poke comp that gets clumped loses to a single AOE engage.
Core pairs seen in the data

Yve + Zhuxin · Pharsa + Brody · Novaria + Kimmy

Loses to

Hard engage (Atlas, Tigreal) and dive assassins. If you are forced into a 5v5 you lose.

Tournament evidence

AG2026: Yve (14 picks, 57.14%) and Zhuxin (12 picks, 50%) are the classic zone-control mids; both were picked heavily in the group stage.

6

Split-push / Tempo Map Pressure

One hero never groups. You win by trading turrets 1-for-1 and forcing the enemy to split, then collapsing.

Win condition Never fight 5v5. Trade structures, force the enemy apart, then punish the split.
Roam
Mathilda, Chip, Angela, Marcel
Mid
Yve, Lylia, Zhuxin, Cecilion
Jungle
Nolan, Ling, Fanny, Hayabusa
EXP
Masha, Sun, Zilong, Aldous, Chou, Aulus
Gold
Claude, Wanwan, Melissa, Brody
How to play it (macro)
  • The split-pusher pushes the lane opposite the current objective — always the far side.
  • The other four stall. Do not fight; just hold the line and make noise.
  • When the enemy sends two to answer the push, collapse 4v3 on the objective.
  • Ward the enemy's rotation path so the pusher never gets collapsed on.
  • Turrets are worth more than kills. Take the tower and leave.
Core pairs seen in the data

Masha + Nolan · Sun + Yve · Aldous + Mathilda

Loses to

Comps that can force a fight you cannot decline, and getting caught 4v5 while the pusher is elsewhere.

Tournament evidence

AG2026: Masha was reworked in patch 2.2.16 from a split-pusher into a sustained-combat bruiser, and was banned 4 times in the bracket — the pure split-push identity has largely left the pro meta.

06Macro — the timeline you actually play

Same six windows every game. What changes between archetypes is what you are trying to do in each one, not the clock.

0:00 – 1:00
Laning setup
Know your win condition before the game starts. Sustain/poke comps farm; pick/dive comps invade the first buff. Do not fight before level 2.
1:00 – 4:00
First Turtle window
First Turtle spawns around 2:00. Setup+dive comps want it; front-to-back comps can concede it. Rotate the mid + roam to the Turtle side before it spawns.
4:00 – 8:00
Rotations and tempo
Clear wave, rotate, repeat. Never stand still. Pick/dive comps hunt isolated targets here; sustain comps just farm and hold.
8:00 – 12:00
Mid game / Lord pressure
Lord spawns around 8:00. This is the decisive window for setup+AOE and sustain comps — both want a fight here, for opposite reasons. Group as five.
12:00 – 16:00
Item spikes
Marksmen and scaling mids hit 3 items. Front-to-back comps become unstoppable now; poke comps must already have a turret lead.
16:00+
Late game / Lord stacking
One mistake ends the game. Never contest Lord without vision. A won Lord fight with a live carry is usually lethal.

Five rules that apply to every archetype

  • Never fight without an objective on the table. A kill with nothing to take is a wasted fight. If Turtle or Lord is not up, you should be farming or trading structures.
  • Fight on your terms or not at all. Setup comps want a grouped 5v5, dive comps want an isolated target, poke comps want nobody near them. If the fight does not match your archetype, disengage.
  • Protect the healer, protect the carry. In sustain and front-to-back comps those two heroes are the win condition. Trading them for a kill is always a bad trade.
  • Vision before objectives. Contesting Lord without a ward on the enemy's approach path is how you lose a 15-minute lead in 20 seconds.
  • Convert, always. Won fight → turret, Turtle or Lord, immediately. The single biggest difference between Mythic and Mythical Glory is what happens in the 10 seconds after a won fight.

07Draft framework — order of operations

1st pickTake your win-condition hero, not the most popular hero. If you are running sustain, take Aulus or the healer; if dive, take the jungler.
2nd–3rd pickComplete your core duo. Do not take two heroes that want the same job — two roamers or two carries loses the draft.
4th–5th pickFill the missing lanes and answer the enemy's threat. Leave the flexible pick last so you can counter.
Ban priorityBan the hero that breaks your archetype, not the strongest hero overall. Sustain comps ban Eudora; setup comps ban Diggie; dive comps ban Lolita.
Always coverOne frontline, one crowd control, one sustained damage, one magic damage, one physical damage. Missing any of these is the most common draft loss.
NeverNever run two heroes with the same weakness. Two squishy carries with no frontline loses to any dive, no matter how good the duos are.

Counter-drafting the archetypes

If they pick…
  • Setup + AOE → take Diggie (strips crowd control) or a split-pusher they cannot force a fight against.
  • Sustain → take burst (Eudora, Gusion, Lancelot) and build anti-heal. Kill before the healing matters.
  • Pick / Dive → take Lolita or Khufra for peel and group tightly. Never travel alone.
  • Front-to-Back → take dive assassins and delete the carry. Their whole comp is one hero.
  • Poke / Siege → take hard engage (Atlas, Tigreal). Force the 5v5 they cannot survive.
  • Split-push → ward the side lane and collapse on the pusher 2v1, then take the 4v3 objective.
Universal ban logic
  • Ban the hero that breaks your archetype, not the strongest hero in the patch. A sustain comp should ban Eudora long before it bans Nolan.
  • In AG2026 the two most-banned heroes were Atlas and Carmilla (19 bans each) — both roamers. The draft war is fought over the roam slot.
  • Nolan was banned only 6 times but picked 23 — teams were willing to play against him. The most-picked hero finished 47.83%, so letting him through was often correct.
  • Ban for the enemy's archetype, not yours. If they are clearly building dive, take Lolita off the board.

08One-page cheat sheet

1. Pick the plan
Setup+AOE · Sustain · Dive · Front-to-Back · Poke · Split-push. Choose one before the draft starts.
2. Lock the core trio
Carry + frontline + jungle. Aulus+Hirara+Obsidia or Atlas+Hirara+Obsidia if the enemy leaves them open.
3. Fill the two open slots
By lane, not by tier list. Every comp needs one frontline, one CC, one sustained damage.
4. Play the clock
Sustain and front-to-back want minute 12+. Dive wants minute 4. Setup wants one fight. Poke wants no fight at all.
5. Fight on your terms
If the fight does not match your archetype, walk away. Losing a fight you should not have taken is the #1 throw.
6. Convert every win
Won fight → turret / Turtle / Lord within 10 seconds. Never reset after a win.

09Methodology

How the trios were derived

  • Start from the 50 measured pairs in Liquipedia's "Played With" tables for the 2026 Asian Games (each pair records how many games two heroes were picked together and how those games ended).
  • Build a graph where heroes are nodes and measured pairs are edges, then find every triangle — three heroes where all three edges exist. A triangle means all three duos genuinely occurred together, so the trio is measured, not inferred.
  • 31 trios emerged. Combined games and combined W–L are the sum across the three component pairs; min pair is the smallest individual pair sample, used as a stability filter.
  • Lanes are the standard pro role for each hero. The "still needs" column lists which of the five jobs the trio does not yet cover.

Caveats

  • 32 games is a small sample. A 10–1 trio has wide error bars — treat the ordering as a strong hint, not a ranking.
  • A triangle proves the three heroes were played together at some point, not that they were picked together in the same game as a deliberate package. It is strong evidence of fit, not proof of intent.
  • Patch context: this is patch 2.2.16 with the Masha and Bruno reworks live. The archetype framework is patch-agnostic; the specific hero recommendations are not.
