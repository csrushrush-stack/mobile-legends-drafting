Read this first — what the numbers are and are not. Ranked win rates come from third-party trackers aggregated over the last 7 days at All Ranks and are not published by Moonton. They are heavily skewed by pick rate: low-pick "pocket" heroes (Rafaela, Lolita, Popol and Kupa) show inflated win rates because only specialists play them. Pro-play figures are exact (match-by-match) and are the most reliable signal in this report. No publisher releases per-duo win rates, so the combination table uses a transparent derived score — see §9 Methodology.

  • Patch 2.2.16 reset the fighter pool. Two reworks (Masha, Bruno) plus 12 buffs pushed Aulus (+2.1 pp), Masha (+2.0 pp) and Argus into the top 5 ranked win rates. Masha's rework is the single biggest meta shift of the patch.
  • Hirara is the patch's problem hero. 66.28% ban rate in ranked and 82.3% contest rate at MSC 2026 — the highest ban priority in both datasets.
  • Ranked and pro meta have diverged sharply. Ranked is dominated by sustain/support (Rafaela, Minotaur, Floryn, Estes). Pro play at M7 was decided by Zhuxin (98.5% contest), Yve (91.2%), Sora (86.9%) and Kalea (86.1%) — almost none of which are ranked standouts. Do not draft off ranked win rates for tournament play.
  • Nerfs hit the marksman pool. Melissa fell −5.6 pp (the largest single drop), with Miya and Hanabi also nerfed. Yi Sun-shin's S1 cooldown nerf dropped him −1.3 pp.
  • The winning draft pattern is unchanged: AOE crowd control + AOE burst. Every S-grade duo in §6 pairs a lockdown engage (Tigreal, Atlas, Khufra, Fredrinn) with a burst follow-up. Diggie is the universal answer to those drafts.

Why some famous combos score badly here. Tigreal + Granger averages 43.76% and Lolita + Moskov 49.37% — yet both are recommended by every guide. The reason is that their component heroes have low solo ranked win rates (Tigreal 44.08%, Granger 43.44%, Lancelot 43.09%, Fanny 42.60%) because they are coordination- and skill-dependent: a random solo-queue Tigreal misses the engage and dies. The combo is strong; the average ranked player piloting half of it is not. Treat the low-scoring combos as high-ceiling, low-floor picks, and the high-scoring ones (Fredrinn + Angela, Yu Zhong + Angela, Terizla + Floryn) as reliable in solo queue. Neither column is a measured duo win rate — use the pro-play contest data in §5 to judge true competitive value.

Counter-play against combos

  • Diggie is the single best answer to every lockdown combo (Tigreal, Atlas, Khufra). His Ultimate removes crowd control from the whole team — if you see an AOE-engage draft, this is the counter-pick.
  • Lolita blocks the projectile half of most poke/burst duos (Granger, Moskov, Gusion, Xavier) and shows up repeatedly in the counter matrix as an answer to marksman duos.
  • Baxia / Akai / Johnson punish the dive duos (Ling + Mathilda, Hayabusa + Mathilda, Nolan + Angela) by denying the exit path.
  • Karrie and X.Borg break the sustain duos (Fredrinn + Angela, Yu Zhong + Angela, Terizla + Floryn) with true damage / HP-based damage.

09Methodology, sources & caveats

Derived metrics

  • Contest % = pick rate + ban rate. Measures how often a hero is removed from the pool either way.
  • Ranked meta score = 0.60 × norm(win rate) + 0.40 × norm(contest), min–max normalised to 0–100 across the 133 heroes. Weights win rate higher because a hero that wins is worth more than one that is merely popular.
  • Pro score = 0.55 × norm(M7 contest) + 0.45 × norm(M7 win rate, min. 10 games), 0–100. Only computed for heroes played at M7.
  • Combo average WR = arithmetic mean of the two heroes' ranked win rates. A derived estimator, not a measured duo win rate.

Why ranked win rates must be read carefully

  • Pick rates are tiny (most heroes under 1%). A 59% win rate on a 1.2% pick rate is a specialist sample, not a broad-population result.
  • All Ranks blends Epic through Mythical Glory. Hero difficulty distorts it: Fanny (42.60%) and Lancelot (43.09%) are strong in expert hands and bad in average ones.
  • Third-party trackers are unofficial and can lag the patch. Patch 2.2.16 landed 16 Sep 2026; this dataset is the trailing 7 days.
