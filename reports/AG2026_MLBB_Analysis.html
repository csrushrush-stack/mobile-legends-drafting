20th Asian Games · Aichi–Nagoya 2026 · MLBB's debut as an official medal event
29 Sep – 1 Oct 2026 Aichi Sky Expo, Tokoname, Japan 11 teams · 32 games Patch 2.2.16 Gold: Myanmar
Data confidence. This is the most reliable dataset in MLBB — official tournament play, every game accounted for. Hero statistics come from Liquipedia's tournament statistics page and reconcile exactly: for all 15 tracked heroes, (picks + bans) ÷ 32 games = the published presence %. That confirms a 32-game sample. Hero pair data comes from Liquipedia's "Played With" tables, which record how often two heroes were picked together and how those games ended — real measured synergy, not an estimate. One caveat: a handful of group-stage matches were not broadcast and have no published draft, so the "heroes used" list may be a few picks short of complete.

  • Myanmar won MLBB's first-ever Asian Games gold medal, sweeping Indonesia 4–0 in a best-of-seven that never looked close. Games ended at 13:13, 14:08, 12:52 and 14:28 — all fast, objective-driven wins. Myanmar's roster is the Yangon Galacticos core; Indonesia fielded the Alter Ego core that finished runner-up at M7.
  • Gloo was the tournament's signature pick. 11 picks, 7–4 (63.64%), and Myanmar won all three games they fielded it in the final. Indonesia only banned it in game four, by which point the series was gone. Notably Gloo won 6–1 on red side but just 1–3 on blue.
  • Nolan was the most-picked hero by a wide margin (23 picks — 72% of all games) yet finished with a losing record at 47.83%. The most-contested hero was not the most valuable one.
  • Valentina went 6–0 (100%) — the highest win rate in the tournament, but on only 6 picks, so treat it as a signal rather than a law.
  • Aulus and Atlas achieved 100% pick/ban presence — they appeared in every single one of the 32 games, either picked or banned.
  • Carmilla was the tournament's biggest trap. 93.75% presence (11 picks, 19 bans) but only 27.27% win rate — teams spent enormous draft capital on a hero that lost.

What the combination data actually says

  • The winning core was Hirara + a lockdown tank. Hirara + Atlas (4–0), Hirara + Obsidia (4–0), Hirara + Aulus (4–1), Hirara + Gloo (3–2), Hirara + Claude (3–0). Hirara individually posted 75.00% — the best win rate of any high-volume hero.
  • Aulus + Eudora went 4–0 — the patch buffed both (Aulus faster ramp-up, Eudora untouched but strong) and the pair converted perfectly.
  • Minotaur anchored cleanly: 3–0 with Eudora, 3–0 with Nolan, 3–0 with Valentina, 3–1 with Aulus.
  • Gloo paired best with Aulus (3–0) and Obsidia (3–0) — frontline-plus-fighter/marksman cores.
  • The worst pairs were real traps. Uranus + Paquito went 0–3, Carmilla + Bruno 0–3, Belerick + Nolan 0–3, and Carmilla + Cecilion only 2–4 despite being played 6 times — the most-played pair in the tournament with a losing record.
  • Most-played pair: Nolan + Gloo and Nolan + Obsidia (6 games each), plus Eudora + Atlas (6) — the Nolan+Gloo pair converted at 66.67%, Eudora+Atlas at 66.67%, but Nolan+Obsidia only 50%.

09Methodology & caveats

How the numbers were derived

  • Total games (32) was reverse-engineered and then verified: for each of the 15 tracked heroes, (picks + bans) ÷ 32 reproduces the published presence percentage exactly. Nolan 29/32 = 90.63%, Aulus 32/32 = 100%, Valentina 12/32 = 37.50%. The fit is perfect across all 15, so 32 is certain.
  • Win rate = wins ÷ picks for that hero. This is a hero's record when picked, not a causal measure — strong teams pick the same heroes, so team quality is baked in.
  • Ban rate = bans ÷ 32. Presence = (picks + bans) ÷ 32.
  • Combination win rate = games won ÷ games played together. Pair data is from Liquipedia's "Played With" tables, which list each hero's top 5 partners. Each pair is reciprocal, so the 50 unique pairs here are deduplicated. Pairs with fewer than 3 games together are statistically noisy.

Known limitations

  • Only 15 heroes have published statistics rows. Many more were picked; for those, only the fact of the pick is recorded, not a win rate.
  • Four group-stage matches were not broadcast and have no published draft (Myanmar–Uzbekistan, Uzbekistan–Hong Kong, Indonesia–Saudi Arabia, Philippines–Jordan). The hero list may therefore be slightly incomplete.
  • Draft attribution in the source markup interleaves both teams' picks within a game block. Individual hero-to-team assignments in the grand-final table follow the published blocks and could be off by a slot in one or two cases.
  • 32 games is a small sample. A 6–0 record (Valentina) or a 3–0 pair carries wide error bars. Do not treat single-digit samples as settled.
