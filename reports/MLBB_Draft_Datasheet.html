Draft Datasheet — Core Trio to Full Five

Pick a high-win-rate core. See exactly who fills the two open lanes. See what to do when the enemy steals a component. Built on the measured 2026 Asian Games dataset (32 games) plus patch 2.2.16 ranked data and the 133-hero counter matrix.
21 core trios 2 open lanes each, ranked T1–T6 Steal + counter contingencies Master ladders for all 5 lanes
How to read the fit score. Each candidate is scored 0.35 × measured synergy (real games played together with your core, from the tournament) + 0.25 × tournament win rate + 0.25 × ranked meta score + 0.15 × ban rate. Where a measured link exists it is printed in the table — that is hard evidence. Where it says no measured link, the hero is ranked purely on patch form, and you should treat it as an informed guess rather than a measured one. The Their answer column is the risk: who the enemy picks to beat your pick.

01The draft flow

1
Lock a core trio first
Pick one of the 21 measured trios below. It covers 3 of the 5 lanes and has a real tournament record. Do not start from a single hero — start from a trio.
2
Read the two open lanes
Each trio card tells you exactly which two lanes are still empty. Those are the only two decisions left.
3
Take T1 for each open lane — unless it is banned
T1 is the best-scoring fit. If the enemy bans or takes it, move straight down the ladder: T2, then T3. Never improvise off the ladder.
4
Check the "their answer" column before you commit
If the enemy has already shown a hero that counters your T1, skip to the next option on the ladder that is not countered by it.
5
If your core itself is broken, switch trios
Losing a core member is not a slot swap — it is a different comp. Use §04 to find the next-best trio that does not use the stolen hero.

06Ban plan

  • Ban the hero that breaks your archetype, not the strongest hero. If you are running sustain, Eudora hurts you far more than Nolan does.
  • In AG2026 the two most-banned heroes were Atlas and Carmilla, 19 bans each — both roamers. The draft war is fought over the roam slot, so protect or deny that slot first.
  • Nolan was banned only 6 times but picked 23 — teams were happy to play against him, and he finished 47.83%. Do not spend a ban on the most-picked hero by default.
  • If your core trio contains a hero with a high ban rate (Hirara 66%, Eudora 60%, Belerick 56%, Paquito 45%, Gloo 38% in ranked), assume it will be taken and pre-plan the T2 option before the draft starts.
  • Diggie is the single best answer to any lockdown comp. If the enemy is building Atlas/Tigreal/Khufra, either ban Diggie or plan around it.

07Methodology

Where each number comes from

  • Core trios — triangles in the measured pair graph from Liquipedia's 2026 Asian Games "Played With" tables. A triangle means all three duos genuinely occurred together, so the trio is measured, not inferred. Only trios whose smallest component pair has 3+ games are shown.
  • Measured link — the actual games and win rate of the candidate hero playing alongside your core members in that tournament. This is the strongest evidence in the sheet.
  • Tournament WR — the hero's record when picked at AG2026. Only 15 heroes have this; the rest are blank.
  • Ranked meta score — from patch 2.2.16 ranked data, 0–100, weighting win rate and pick+ban presence.
  • Countered by / Strong against — the 133-hero Season 42 counter matrix.

Caveats

  • 32 tournament games is a small sample. A 12–2 trio is strong evidence but not certainty.
  • Most side-lane candidates have no measured link to a given trio. Those rows are patch-form inferences, clearly labelled — do not read them as measured synergy.
  • The counter matrix is ranked-play data, not pro-play data. Pro teams exploit counters more consistently than ranked does.
  • Patch-specific: hero names reflect patch 2.2.16 with the Masha and Bruno reworks live. The method carries forward; the hero lists will not.
