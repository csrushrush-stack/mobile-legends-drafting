How the recommendations work How the recommendations work. Each candidate is scored on: lane fit (does it fill a lane you still need) · measured synergy (real games it played alongside your picks at the 2026 Asian Games) · matchup (how many of the enemy's picks it beats, minus how many beat it) · pro win rate (its actual record across the selected leagues) · ranked meta (patch 2.2.16) · team balance (you need a frontline and both damage types).

Lineup win % = the mean pro win rate of your five heroes, so it answers "how proven is this lineup". vs their lineup = a matchup estimate from the 133-hero counter matrix, capped at 30–70% because it is an estimate, not a measurement. Both are shown on every recommendation.

What each source is. AG 2026 is a closed 32-game snapshot of the Asian Games only — it never changes and is not affected by league updates. AG + MPL pools the Asian Games with every current regional league (960 games) and is the one that updates as leagues finish. Ranked + All adds the publisher's own Mythical Glory+ numbers on top, and switches the draft to the ranked ban/pick order. The three draft rules apply to the two professional sources only.

Where the data comes from. Ranked win / pick / ban rates are the publisher's own figures, pulled from the public endpoint behind mobilelegends.com/rank filtered to Mythical Glory+ (133 heroes, patch 2.2.16). Professional records come from the tournament statistics pages on Liquipedia: Asian Games 2026 (32 games), MPL Philippines S17 (169), MPL Indonesia S17 (205), MPL Malaysia S17 (164), MSL Myanmar S4 (86), MTC Türkiye S7 (142) and MCC S7 CIS/EECA (162) — 960 games in total. Counter matchups come from a 133-hero ranked counter matrix.

Tiers come from your own list, not from the data. The ranked ladder and tournament priority disagree sharply — Claude has a 0.07% ranked ban rate but was picked in 52% of all 960 professional games, while Lukas has a 67% ranked ban rate and 36 professional games. Your list drives the rules, flex detection and lane coverage. The ban-rate-derived tier is shown alongside as a comparison only.
