How the recommendations work. Each candidate is scored on: lane fit (does it fill a lane you still need) · measured synergy (real games it played alongside your picks at the 2026 Asian Games) · matchup (how many of the enemy's picks it beats, minus how many beat it) · pro win rate (its actual record across the selected leagues) · ranked meta (patch 2.2.16) · team balance (you need a frontline and both damage types).

Lineup win % = the mean pro win rate of your five heroes, so it answers "how proven is this lineup". vs their lineup = a matchup estimate from the 133-hero counter matrix, capped at 30–70% because it is an estimate, not a measurement. Both are shown on every recommendation.

Switch the data source at the top: Asian Games 2026 uses only the 32-game international dataset, while AG + MPL pools every current league. Small samples are labelled — a hero with fewer than 3 pro games is flagged as unproven rather than scored highly.
